# Code of conduct

This code applies to every AllsWeb repository and to its issues, pull requests and
discussions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient. Assume the other person means well.
- Keep discussion on the technical subject. Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Accept that maintainers decide what is merged, and that they may say no.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising and repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that
break this code. To report a problem, write to **hi@allsweb.com**. Reports are kept
confidential.
